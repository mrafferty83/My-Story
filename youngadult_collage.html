Young Adult Life

During grade school I played hockey and ran track. In high school, I joined JROTC, which took up all my extracurricular time. After graduating from high school, I enlisted in the Marine Corps and graduated Bootcamp in Dec 2000. I primarily served in Golf 2/7, 1st MarDiv, but did some augmentation deployments with 1/6 and 2/5. It was the best and worst time of my life — I am grateful for the experiences and friends I made, but it was extremely difficult, and even now, 20 years later, I occasionally still struggle with that period of time.
